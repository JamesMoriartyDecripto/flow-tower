# Halden Bikes: brand voice guide

Halden builds electric cargo bikes in Amsterdam for families and small businesses in NL, BE and DE. We sound like the friend who fixed your bike and then stayed for coffee: practical, warm, a little dry.

## Voice

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Practical: show the school run, the rain, the crate of groceries | Aspirational lifestyle with no cargo in the box |
| Warm and direct, "you" and "we" | Corporate ("our valued customers") |
| Dry humour about weather and parking | Sarcasm about other road users or car drivers |
| Specific: numbers with a source | Superlatives ("the best", "the safest") |

Reading level: plain language (B1). English by default; Dutch and German variants are written natively, never machine-translated without a native reviewer.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)

1. **Real riders** (35%): customer stories, UGC with written permission.
2. **How-to** (25%): maintenance, child seats, winter riding, charging.
3. **Behind the workshop** (20%): build, test rig, people.
4. **City cycling** (20%): infrastructure, events, policy news we can add something useful to.

Product promotion lives inside these pillars; no more than one direct sales post in five.

## Platform notes

- **Instagram / Facebook:** Reels first, carousels for how-to. Captions under 125 characters before "more".
- **TikTok:** native, face-to-camera, sound on. Finished in the app by a person.
- **LinkedIn:** the company story: hiring, B2B fleet customers, workshop numbers.
- **YouTube Shorts:** how-to clips with a link to the long video.
- **Threads / Bluesky:** conversational, questions to the community, replies matter more than posts.
- **X:** short updates and service notices only; no links in the post (cost and reach).

## Visual rules

- Real product photos for any claim about the product. Generated images only for backgrounds, mood and concepts, and they get the AI flag.
- Never generate a realistic person, a real place presented as real, or a customer.
- Colours and type come from the Canva brand kit; logo bottom-right on Instagram only, never on TikTok.

## Replies

- Answer the person, not the crowd. Use their first name if public.
- Acknowledge first, then the next concrete step. One apology, no excuses.
- Order, warranty or safety: move to DM or the helpdesk; never discuss order details in public.
- Sign replies from people with initials (" - MV"). Agents never sign.
