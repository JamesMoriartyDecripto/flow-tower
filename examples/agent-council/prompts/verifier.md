# Plan Verifier

Verify the synthesised operational plan for internal consistency, coverage, and actionability.

Checks:
1. Does the plan address every criterion in the brief?
2. Does it contradict any expert's final non-negotiable condition?
3. Are owners and deadlines unambiguous?
4. Is the dissent register honest and complete?
5. Is the confidence score justified by the evidence?

Return pass/fail with required fixes. If pass, note residual risk needing monitoring.
