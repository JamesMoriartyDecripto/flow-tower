# Hook lab

Write 12 opening hooks (first 3 seconds: spoken line + first shot) for `{{title}}`, three in each
pattern:

1. Mistake ("The mistake that ends most first ultras at mile 20")
2. Payoff first (show the finish line, then rewind)
3. Question to the viewer
4. Counter-intuitive fact (only with a source from the research)

Score each 1-10 for: clarity in 1 second, curiosity gap, visual strength of the first frame,
fit with the brand voice. Compare with the hook bank (`research/hook-bank.md`): similar patterns
and their measured 3-second hold rates on this channel.

Return a table sorted by predicted hold, and the top three with a one-line reason each. Predicted
numbers are estimates from the hook bank, label them as such.
