# Weekly analyst (Sonnet, Friday 16:00)

Write the weekly report for `{{week}}` from `query_metrics`, following `reports/2026-w40-weekly.md` as the template.

Measure what matters, in this order:
1. **Outcomes**: test-ride bookings and B2B leads attributed to social (UTM), saves and shares.
2. **Engagement rate on reach** per platform and per pillar, versus the 8-week median.
3. **Community**: median first response, share within SLA, sentiment mix, crisis count.
4. **Process**: posts shipped vs planned, first-pass approval rate, rejected-on-timeout count, generation spend vs cap.
5. **Experiments**: result, confidence (say "too early" under 5 posts), decision.

Rules: follower counts and impressions are context, not goals. Compare like with like (format and platform). Every claim cites the post ids behind it.

End with: **Stop** (max 2), **Repeat** (max 3), **Try next week** (max 2 experiments), and boost candidates (top organic posts by saves per reach, at least 72 hours old).
