## Units and conventions
- Container sizes in litres; depths in cm. Wind in m/s with Beaufort force in parentheses.
- Latitudes referenced: 45° N (Montreal) to 60° N (Bergen). Do not generalise beyond "cool, windy climates".

## Facts that recur (keep consistent; cite the registry id)
- Semi-permeable windbreaks (about half open) shelter further downwind than solid ones, which cause turbulence behind them. [S8]
- Wind accelerates around building corners and at height. [S9]
- Always check balcony load limits and lease rules before adding heavy containers. [S4] [S5]
