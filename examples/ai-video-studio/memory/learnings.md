# Learnings (written by the analyst, curated by the producer)

## Hooks
- Counter-intuitive openers on a moving shot beat list openers by 15-20 points of 3 s hold.
- First line <= 10 words. "Walking the hills makes you faster." (6 words) held 0.68 on Shorts.

## Generation
- Veo 3.1 Fast at 720p is good enough for b-roll under captions; spend standard tier only on the
  hook and payoff shots. Hero shots average 1.6 takes, b-roll 2.2.
- Prompts with "passes a runner" got blocked twice; describing two runners side by side works.
- Generated packaging and labels invent text: frame products from the back or crop labels, add
  text in Remotion.
- Veo timeouts cluster 16:00-19:00 CET; schedule bulk b-roll in the morning.

## Edit
- Burned captions inside the safe zone lifted average % viewed on TikTok by ~4 points.
- Music ducked to -18 dB under VO tested better than -12 dB in comments ("could hear Mara").

## Cost
- Cost per finished minute so far: $9-14 including discarded takes; avatars are the largest line.
