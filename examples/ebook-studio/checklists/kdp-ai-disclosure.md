## 2. Amazon KDP (Content Guidelines, AI content)
- [x] KDP defines AI-generated content as text, images or translations created by an AI-based tool, **even if substantial edits were applied afterwards**. AI-drafted sections therefore count, even though the author rewrote them.
- [x] AI-assisted content (edited, refined, checked with AI) does not need to be disclosed. Disclosed anyway where it overlaps.
- [x] Answer the AI questions per content type: text **yes**, images **no**, translations **no**.
- [x] Disclosure is required again when an edited book is republished (v1.0.x updates included).
- [x] We remain responsible for all AI-generated and AI-assisted content meeting the content guidelines (no misleading description, no copied text).
- [x] Volume limit (3 new titles per day per account, reported 2023) not relevant for one title.

## 3. Other stores
- [x] **Apple Books** (Formatting and Content Guidelines 1.13, 2.9, 4.8): AI-generated content needs the "AI Generated by" artist role and a line in the description. Added: role + "Parts of the text were drafted with AI from the author's notes and rewritten by the author."
- [x] **Kobo Writing Life**: declines books wholly or partially AI-generated without human effort. The editorial record (ledger, editorial letters, author rewrite history) is kept on file.
- [x] **Draft2Digital**: accepts AI-assisted content and AI text with extensive human editing; refuses text generated entirely by AI without it. Passes.
- [x] **Google Play Books**: no AI-specific rule found; quality rules apply.

## 4. Law and labels
- [x] **EU AI Act, Article 50(4)** (applies from 2 August 2026): labelling of AI-generated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest; not required where the text has had human review or editorial control **and** a person holds editorial responsibility. A practical gardening book is unlikely to be in scope; the copyright page still names Larchwood Press as editorially responsible.
- [x] **US Copyright Office** (Copyright and AI, Part 2, January 2025): purely AI-generated material is not protected; prompts alone are not enough; human selection, arrangement and modification can be. If registering in the US, disclose and disclaim AI-generated material that is more than de minimis (the 6 lightly edited sections).
- [x] **Authors Guild "Human Authored"** mark: not eligible (AI-generated text beyond de minimis). Do not use the mark or similar wording in marketing.
