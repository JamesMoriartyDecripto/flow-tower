# Storyboard: Your First Ultra (9:16 short + 16:9 master)

Look: late-afternoon light, slate and ember palette, light grain, no lens flares. Runners are
non-identifiable (back, side, distance, buff over the face) except Mara. Product shots use the
Ridgeline 12L packshots as references.

| Shot | Beat | Kind | Frame | Camera | Continuity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | Hook / mistake | t2v (hero) | Runner sitting on a granite rock at a ridge, head down, sunset behind | Slow push-in, 35 mm feel | Ember vest, grey shorts |
| S02 | Not fitness | avatar | Mara mid-shot at a trailhead sign (no text on sign) | Locked off | Slate jacket, hair tied |
| G01 | Three things | graphic | Three numbered cards on slate | Remotion spring-in | Brand fonts |
| S03 | Pacing | t2v | Drone over 40 runners leaving a start arch, no logos | Drone tracking back | Morning light (flashback) |
| S04 | Walk the hills | t2v | Runner power-hiking, hands on knees, passing a runner who is jogging | Low side tracking | Ember vest |
| S05 | Fuelling | i2v (product) | Hand pulls a gel from the vest's front pocket | Macro, shallow focus | Packshot keyframe, ember colourway |
| S06 | Kit | i2v (product) | Vest on runner's back, bounce-free on a descent | Follow from behind | Slate colourway |
| S07 | CTA | avatar | Mara at the trailhead, points down to link | Locked off | Same as S02 |

Rules carried into the shot list: 8 s for any 1080p shot on Veo 3.1; 9:16 and 16:9 rendered
separately for hero shots (S01, S04), centre-safe framing for the rest so one 16:9 render can be
cropped.
