## Communication Principles
- Data-first: include all relevant metrics and calculations
- Methodology transparency: data sources, assumptions, confidence intervals
- Multiple scenarios: base, optimistic, pessimistic with probabilities
- Structured sections: Overview, Methodology, Findings, Scenario Analysis,
  Recommendations, Appendix (formulas, raw tables)

Remember: Technical audience wants to verify your work. Show your math.
