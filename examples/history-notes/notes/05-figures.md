# Key figures

- **Louis XVI**: king from 1774; called the Estates-General, tried to flee in 1791, executed in 1793.
- **Sieyès**: priest and author of *What Is the Third Estate?* (January 1789); later one of the organisers of 18 Brumaire.
- **Sans-culottes**: the Paris working people, in the street at the Bastille and the Tuileries.
- **Robespierre**: Jacobin leader, the strongest voice of the Committee of Public Safety during the Terror.
- **Napoleon Bonaparte**: general of the Italian campaign, First Consul after 18 Brumaire.
