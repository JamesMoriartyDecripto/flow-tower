# From constitutional monarchy to Republic

- **July 1790**: the **Civil Constitution of the Clergy** puts the Church under the state and splits the clergy.
- **June 1791**: the royal family flees and is stopped at **Varennes**. Trust in the king collapses.
- **September 1791**: the **Constitution of 1791**: constitutional monarchy, Legislative Assembly.
- **April 1792**: war on Austria (Prussia joins).
- **10 August 1792**: the Tuileries palace is stormed; the king is suspended.
- **September 1792**: the National Convention abolishes the monarchy and proclaims the **Republic**.
- **21 January 1793**: **Louis XVI is executed**.
