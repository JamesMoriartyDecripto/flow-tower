# Pitch - Emberwake

**Hook:** Rekindle the last beacons of a drifting sky-archipelago with up to three friends,
before the cold dark swallows the islands for good.

**Genre:** Third-person co-op (1-4) survival-crafting roguelite. Runs of 30-45 minutes.

**Fantasy:** You are a Keeper, a lamplighter whose lantern is both your light and your life.
Each run generates a new archipelago of floating islands. Grapple between islands, gather
driftwood and ember-ore, craft gear, fend off Cinder Wisps, and rekindle beacons to push the
dark back. Rekindled beacons persist as shortcuts and unlocks for the next run.

**Core loop:** explore -> gather -> craft -> rekindle -> push deeper -> extract or fall.

**Pillars:** warm light in a cold world; traversal is joy; together or not at all.

**Audience:** co-op players who like Driftlands and Lantern Rogue; streamers; 18-35.

**Comps:** Driftlands, Lantern Rogue, Skyward Salvage (see memory/competitors.md).

**Scope ask:** a 20-minute vertical slice of one biome (Ashen Shoals) in 10 weeks,
PC/Steam, Unreal Engine 5.6, plus a coming-soon store page and announcement trailer.

**Budget ask:** $1,200 in model spend, 3 human checkpoints.

**Known risks:** co-op netcode, procedural reachability, "ember" naming collisions.
