# Work and energy

- **Work**, in joules; zero when the force is perpendicular to the motion:

$$
W = F\,s\cos\theta
$$

- **Power** $$P = \dfrac{W}{t}$$, in watts.
- **Kinetic energy** $$K = \tfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2$$.
- **Work-energy theorem**: the work of all forces equals the change in kinetic energy, $$W_{tot} = \Delta K$$.
- **Potential energy**: gravitational $$U = m\,g\,h$$, elastic $$U = \tfrac{1}{2}\,k\,x^2$$.

## Conservation

With only conservative forces (weight, springs) the mechanical energy stays constant:

$$
E = K + U = \text{constant}
$$

With friction, the mechanical energy lost equals the (negative) work of friction:

$$
\Delta E = W_{friction}
$$
