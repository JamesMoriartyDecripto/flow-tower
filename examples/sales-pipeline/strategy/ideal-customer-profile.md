# Ideal customer profile: Ferrovento S.r.l. (fictional)

Version 2026-Q4 · owner: Head of Sales · approved 2026-10-06 · evidence: 31 won and 58 lost
deals since 2024-01 (HubSpot export, `analytics.mart`).

## What we sell

| Offer | Shape | Typical price |
|---|---|---|
| Data Platform Starter | Fixed price, 8-10 weeks: ERP + MES extraction, cloud warehouse, 5 dashboards | 28,000-45,000 EUR |
| Integration Sprint | Fixed price, 3-4 weeks: one ERP/MES/e-commerce integration | 9,500-16,000 EUR |
| Data Ops Retainer | Monthly, 3-month minimum: monitoring, changes, 2 days of analyst time | 3,200-5,800 EUR/month |

Average won deal (first 12 months): 46,300 EUR. Median cycle: 61 days (illustrative).

## Segments (in priority order)

1. **IT-MFG-EMR**: Italian discrete manufacturers in Emilia-Romagna, Lombardy, Veneto.
   ATECO 25 (metal products), 27 (electrical equipment), 28 (machinery). 50-500 employees,
   turnover 10-120 M EUR. Win rate 34 %.
2. **IT-FOOD**: Italian food and beverage producers (ATECO 10, 11), 80-400 employees.
   Win rate 22 %; longer cycles (seasonality, family ownership).
3. **FR-MFG**: French manufacturers (NAF 25, 27, 28), 100-500 employees, Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes
   and Grand Est, with an Italian parent or supplier relationship. Win rate 19 %.

## Buying triggers (observable, dated)

- ERP migration announced or under way (job posts for SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics, TeamSystem).
- New plant, line or acquisition in the last 9 months (press, company website).
- Hiring a first "data analyst" or "controller industriale" role.
- Transizione 5.0 investment projects (energy monitoring needs plant data).

## Personas

| Persona | Role | What they care about | Typical objection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | CEO / owner, CFO | Margin per order, cash, not depending on one IT person | "We already have the ERP reports" |
| Champion | Controller industriale, plant manager | Monthly close in days not weeks, OEE truth | "No time for a project now" |
| Technical evaluator | IT manager (often a team of 1-3) | Not breaking the ERP, who maintains it after | "Security / where is our data?" |

## Disqualifiers

- Fewer than 30 employees or no ERP in place.
- Public administration (tender process, different sales motion).
- Companies in insolvency proceedings (Atoka flag) or under a current Ferrovento NDA conflict.
- Existing customers of a partner we have a non-solicit agreement with (list in the source register).

## What changed this quarter

- Food segment moved from 1st to 2nd: lost 6 of 8 deals in Q3 to "budget frozen until harvest".
- Added the Transizione 5.0 trigger: 4 of the last 7 wins mentioned it on the first call.
