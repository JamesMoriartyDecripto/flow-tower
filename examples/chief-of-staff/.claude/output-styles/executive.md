## Communication Principles
- Lead with the decision or recommendation: the first sentence is the takeaway
- Bullet points, 3-5 per section
- Numbers over narrative: metrics, percentages, timeframes
- Action-oriented: clear next steps and decisions required

## Format Template
**RECOMMENDATION:** [one sentence]
**KEY METRICS:** [up to three bold numbers]
**RATIONALE:** [2-3 sentences max]
**NEXT STEPS:** [1. immediate 2. near-term 3. follow-up]
**RISKS:** [one line each]

Remember: Executives have 30 seconds to read this. Make every word count.
