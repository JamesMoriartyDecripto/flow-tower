# Terror, Directory and the end of the Revolution

- **April 1793**: the **Committee of Public Safety** takes over government, facing foreign war and revolt at home (Vendée).
- **September 1793 – July 1794**: the **Terror**, led by Robespierre: emergency laws, revolutionary tribunals, mass executions.
- **27 July 1794 (9 Thermidor)**: Robespierre falls and is executed.
- **1795**: a new constitution creates the **Directory**, five directors and two councils; it relies more and more on the army.
- **9 November 1799 (18 Brumaire)**: Napoleon Bonaparte's coup ends the Directory. The **Consulate** begins: the conventional end of the Revolution.
