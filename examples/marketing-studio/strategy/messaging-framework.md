# Messaging framework — Q4 2026 (approved 2026-10-06 by Head of PMM)

## Positioning statement
For mid-market finance teams in Europe that process thousands of supplier invoices a month in
SAP, Dynamics or Exact, Tallymoor is the accounts-payable automation layer that captures,
routes and posts invoices without re-keying, so the AP team closes on time without adding
headcount. Unlike suite AP modules, it works across ERPs and supplier formats from day one.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Promise | Proof (claim ids) | Persona |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. No more re-keying | Invoices arrive as data, not PDFs | C-001 capture accuracy, C-004 e-invoice formats | AP lead |
| 2. Approvals that move | Every invoice has an owner and a deadline | C-002 cycle time, C-006 audit trail | Controller, AP lead |
| 3. Grow without hiring | Volume grows, the team does not | C-003 invoices per FTE, C-005 payback | CFO |

## Objection handling
- **"Another tool to learn."** Approvers act from email or Teams; only AP uses the app. (C-006)
- **"Our supplier formats are a mess."** Show the format list and the fallback queue. (C-004)
- **"Mid-migration."** Connectors for both old and new ERP during the overlap.
- **"Data residency."** EU-only hosting, DPA and sub-processor list on the trust page. (C-008)

## Voice
Plain, specific, numerate. Finance people distrust adjectives.
- Say: "invoices posted without re-keying", "approval overdue after 3 days".
- Avoid: "revolutionary", "seamless", "AI magic", "effortless", "best-in-class".
- AI: describe what it does ("reads line items from PDFs and XRechnung files"), not that it is AI.

## Message by funnel stage
- **Problem-aware:** the cost of re-keying and stuck approvals (articles, LinkedIn).
- **Solution-aware:** AP automation vs. suite modules vs. BPO (comparison pages, search).
- **Product-aware:** ERP integration pages, ROI calculator, demo.
