# Competitors & market notes

Maintained by the research department. Each claim carries a source and a date.
Figures are estimates from public store data and review counts, refreshed every milestone.

## Positioning signals
- Co-op survival-crafting with short (30-45 min) roguelite runs is under-served; most comps are sandbox.
- "Cozy but tense" tags (co-op, atmospheric, roguelite) trend upward in wishlist proxies.
- Price band for a 4-player indie co-op at launch: $19.99-$24.99.

## Risks
- Name collision risk with "ember" titles; trademark search is on the licence checklist.
- Procedural generation is a weak hook alone; lead with traversal and rekindling moments.
