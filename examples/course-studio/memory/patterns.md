# Learned patterns

Promoted by the pipeline when a finding recurs in 2+ courses. Reviewed in PRs.
Sections tagged `[flag]` are injected only when the brief has that risk flag.

## Always
- DO write the evidence plan before the outline. Courses designed content-first failed alignment in round 1 (3 of 4 cases).
- DO give each module ONE fictional company and keep names consistent; switching mid-module confused 2 of 3 personas.
- DON'T put the narration on the slide. Pedagogy reviewer flagged redundancy in every course that did.
- DON'T let writers add facts not in the research pack. 80% of fact-check blockers were uncited additions.

## Pedagogy
- DO end every lesson with a "use it this week" task; SMEs consistently asked for it.
- DO keep worked examples to 3-5 steps; longer ones were skipped by the novice persona.
- DON'T write "Remember" objectives for practitioner courses unless a later objective depends on them.

## Review
- DO pass previous blockers back to the same reviewer; it halved round-2 re-raises.
- DON'T let the consolidator downgrade accessibility blockers. One course shipped a captionless video that way (fixed in the gate since).

## Assessment
- DO derive distractors from misconceptions named in the research pack. Generic distractors had p > 0.9 in pilots.
- DON'T use "which is NOT" stems; the ESL persona misread them every time.

## [fast_moving]
- DO date every version-sensitive claim ("as of October 2026") and set freshness to 6 months.
- DO prefer vendor docs via Context7 over blog posts for API behaviour.

## [localization]
- DO lock product terms in the glossary before writing; retrofitting terms cost a full localization pass.
- DON'T localize captions cue by cue; re-segment for the target language.

## [third_party_ip]
- DO record licence and reuse mode at source time. Fixing licences after writing cost a full review round.
