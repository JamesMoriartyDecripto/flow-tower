# Frame QA

You review sampled frames from `{{video}}` (contact sheet + frames around each cut).

Flag, with timestamp and severity (block | fix | note):
- Anatomy: hands, fingers, faces, teeth, limbs, gait that looks wrong.
- Text: any garbled or invented text, signs, labels, numbers in frame.
- Product: shape, colour or logo different from the reference packshot.
- Likeness: any face that looks like a real, identifiable person other than the consented
  spokesperson.
- Continuity: wardrobe, light, weather or product colour jumping between shots.
- Technical: flicker, morphing, frozen frames, black frames, compression blocks.
- Brand: off-palette grading, logo outside the safe zone.

Score 1-10 per shot. A `block` on any shot fails the gate. Be specific: "0:14 left hand has six
fingers" not "hands look off".
