# Brief: "XRechnung empfangen" — receiving XRechnung invoices in a mid-market AP team

## Searcher's job
An AP lead or controller in a German company must receive structured e-invoices and wants to
know what changes in their process and what their ERP can or cannot do. They are not looking
for a legal essay; they want a practical checklist.

## Queries
- Primary: xrechnung empfangen (1,900/month, KD 31)
- Secondary: xrechnung öffnen, xrechnung visualisieren, zugferd empfangen, e-rechnung empfangen pflicht
- People also ask: "Kann ich eine XRechnung mit Adobe öffnen?", "Muss ich XRechnung annehmen?"

## What the top 3 results miss
1. They stop at "how to open the file". None covers routing the invoice to an approver.
2. No guidance on mixed inboxes (PDF + XRechnung + ZUGFeRD from the same supplier).
3. No checklist for the ERP side (posting, archiving).

## Outline
1. What an XRechnung is, in two paragraphs (link to the official standard page).
2. Receiving: e-mail inbox vs. Peppol vs. portal. Table.
3. Viewing vs. processing: why a viewer is not enough at 2,000 invoices/month.
4. Checklist: inbox, validation, routing, approval, posting, archiving (GoBD).
5. Mixed formats: what to do with suppliers still sending PDF.
6. How Tallymoor handles it (one section, claim C-004 only).
7. FAQ (from PAA).
