# Solving a mechanics problem

1. **Read** the text twice. Underline the data and the question.
2. **Data and unknowns**: write every quantity with its symbol and convert it to SI units (m, kg, s).
3. **Sketch**: draw the situation; for forces, a free-body diagram with one arrow per force.
4. **Choose the approach**:
   - only positions, speeds and times → kinematics;
   - forces are given or asked → Newton's laws;
   - heights and speeds, no time asked → energy (often the shortest path).
5. **Solve with symbols first**, put numbers in at the end.
6. **Check**: units must match on both sides; the size of the result must make sense (a car at $$900\ \text{m/s}$$ is wrong). If not, go back to step 5.
