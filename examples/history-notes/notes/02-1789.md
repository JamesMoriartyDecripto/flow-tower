# 1789: the Constituent Assembly

- **Vote by order or by head?** By order, the two privileged Estates outvote the Third 2 to 1. Deadlock.
- **17 June**: the Third Estate declares itself the **National Assembly**.
- **20 June**: locked out of its hall, it swears the **Tennis Court Oath**: no disbanding until France has a constitution.
- **14 July**: Paris crowds storm the **Bastille**, symbol of royal power.
- Summer: the **Great Fear**, peasant revolts against the manors.
- **4 August**: the Assembly abolishes feudal privileges.
- **26 August**: **Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen**.
