# Kinematics: describing motion

- Position $$x(t)$$, displacement $$\Delta x = x_2 - x_1$$.
- Average velocity, in m/s:

$$
v = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}
$$

- Average acceleration, in m/s²:

$$
a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}
$$

## Uniform motion ($$a = 0$$)

$$
x = x_0 + v\,t
$$

## Uniformly accelerated motion ($$a$$ constant)

$$
\begin{aligned}
v &= v_0 + a\,t \\
x &= x_0 + v_0\,t + \tfrac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2 \\
v^2 &= v_0^2 + 2\,a\,\Delta x \quad \text{(no time needed)}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Free fall

Uniformly accelerated motion with $$a = g \approx 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$$ downward, air resistance ignored.
