# Hook bank: Kestrel channel (fictional data)

Measured 3-second hold rate = share of viewers still watching at 3 s (YouTube Shorts "stayed to
watch" is not identical; we compute ours from the retention curve). Values are this channel's,
not benchmarks.

| Pattern | Example hook (first line) | First frame | Videos | Median 3 s hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mistake | "This mistake ends most first ultras at mile 20." | Runner sitting on a rock, head down | 9 | 0.69 |
| Payoff first | "She finished her first 50K. Here's the one thing she changed." | Finish arch, arms up | 7 | 0.66 |
| Question | "How many gels do you need for a 50K?" | Gels fanned out on a table | 8 | 0.58 |
| Counter-intuitive | "Walking the hills makes you faster." | Runner power-hiking past a runner | 6 | 0.71 |
| List | "3 things I wish I knew before my first ultra." | Text on black | 10 | 0.52 |

Notes
- Text-on-black openers underperform on this channel by ~15 points; always open on a moving shot.
- Faces in the first frame helped only when the person was mid-action, not posing.
- Hooks longer than 12 words lost 6-9 points.
