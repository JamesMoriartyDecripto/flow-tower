## 2. Tax credits and incentives

### 2.1 Iperammortamento 2026-2028 ("Nuovo Piano Transizione 5.0 – Iperammortamento")
- **Who:** business-income subjects (see 1.1). Requires a taxable profit to use it (losses defer the benefit). **CPB adherents can use it**: L. 88/2026 (conversion of DL 38/2026, art. 7/7-bis) — FiscoOggi says the uplift does not count in the agreed income for investments 1/1/2026 – 30/9/2028 (exact mechanism: verify text).
- **Size:** +180% / +100% / +50% by bracket (≤2.5 m, 2.5-10 m, 10-20 m). Whether the brackets are annual or cumulative over 2026-2028 is not stated in the law (Confindustria).
- **Goods:** new goods in Allegati IV (tangible 4.0, now including AI/HPC, cybersecurity appliances, private 5G, edge computing, energy management) and V (intangible: software, digital product passport, low-code, data spaces), interconnected; or renewable self-production plants with storage (PV modules per art. 12 c.1 lett. b-c DL 181/2023). **The EU/EEA origin requirement was removed by DL 38/2026 art. 7 from 1/1/2026**, confirmed in L. 88/2026.
- **Procedure:** GSE platform only. DM MIMIT-MEF signed 4 or 7 May 2026 (sources differ). Prior communication open since 12/6/2026 (DD MIMIT 10/6/2026); confirmation within 60 days of the GSE OK with ≥20% advance paid (open since 21/7/2026, DD 20/7/2026); completion by 15/11/2028 with perizia asseverata and certificazione contabile (completion function reportedly opens 15/10/2026 — Assolombarda, M); periodic reports by 20 January and 30 June each year.
- **Not cumulable** with the 4.0 credit of L. 207/2024 c. 446 on the same goods; cumulable with other aid on different cost shares. Recapture if goods are sold or moved abroad before the end of depreciation unless replaced (draft decree may be stricter — Confindustria).
- **Source:** art. 1 c. 427-436 L. 199/2025; DL 38/2026 art. 7 conv. L. 88/2026.
- **URLs:** https://www.fiscooggi.it/portale/-/legge-di-bilancio-2026-e-imprese- ; https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/incentivi/piano-transizione-5-0 (points to /it/incentivi/iperammortamento-2026) ; https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/?p=152808 ; https://www.edotto.com/articolo/iperammortamento-2026-sportello-gse-dal-12-giugno-regole-e-domande ; https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/?p=151454
- **Status:** active. **Conf.: H** (rates, dates, goods); M (procedure dates, CPB mechanism).

### 2.2 Transizione 5.0 credit (DL 19/2024 art. 38)
- **Who/size:** credit for 2024-2025 investments in 4.0 goods with energy saving ≥3% (site) or ≥5% (process); brackets modified by L. 207/2024 c. 427-429.
- **Status 2026:** **closed**. Funds exhausted (DD MIMIT 6/11/2025); last bookings 31/12/2025; completion communications for 2025 investments by 28/2/2026. For the waiting-list "esodati": DL 38/2026 art. 8 granted a share of the requested credit (35%), raised by **DL 42/2026 to 89.77%** (EUR 1,302.3 m); MIMIT notice 29/4/2026; credit usable **only in F24 by 31/12/2026** (code 7079), not carried forward, not taxable. DL 175/2025 art. 1 c. 2: no 4.0+5.0 cumulation on the same goods (option by 27/11/2025).
- **URLs:** https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/incentivi/piano-transizione-5-0 ; https://ance.it/2026/04/d-l-42-2026-transizione-5-0-per-il-2025-con-aumento-del-credito-dimposta/ ; https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/fisco-e-patrimonio/reddito-impresa-e-irap/il-credito-dimposta-degli-esodati-transizione-5-0-profili-contabili-e-dichiarativi-dopo-la-conversione-del-d-l-n-38-2026/
- **Lever now:** an "esodato" company must use the credit in F24 by 31/12/2026 or lose it. **Conf.: H** (closure, dates), M (89.77%, code 7079).

### 2.3 Transizione 4.0 credit for beni strumentali (L. 178/2020 c. 1051-1063, as amended by L. 207/2024 c. 445-448)
- **Size (2025):** tangible 4.0 goods (Allegato A L. 232/2016): 20% up to 2.5 m, 10% 2.5-10 m, 5% 10-20 m. Intangible 4.0 (Allegato B): no new investments from 1/1/2025 unless booked in 2024. Overall cap EUR 2.2 bn for 2025 (reported; **UNVERIFIED** in final text).

No new 4.0 credit for 2026 orders: replaced by the iperammortamento. Credit usable in F24 in the following years (3 annual instalments in the 2020-2025 regime: not re-verified).
- **URLs:** Confindustria 03/2026 (p. 57) ; https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/incentivi/piano-transizione-5-0

### 2.4 ZES Unica Mezzogiorno credit (DL 124/2023 art. 16)
- **Who:** companies investing in production structures in Campania, Puglia, Basilicata, Calabria, Sicilia, Sardegna, Molise, Abruzzo and, from 2026, **Marche and Umbria** (only 107.3.c areas; L. 18/11/2025 n. 171). Investments ≥ EUR 200,000 (DM 17/5/2024 regime; threshold not re-verified for 2026: M).
- **Size:** regional-aid intensities, e.g. investments ≤ EUR 50 m: Calabria/Campania/Puglia/Sicilia 40% large, 50% medium, 60% small; Basilicata/Molise/Sardegna 30/40/50%; Abruzzo/Marche/Umbria 15/25/35%; JTF areas (Sulcis, Taranto) 50/60/70%. Budget 2026 EUR 2.3 bn (1 bn 2027, 0.75 bn 2028), pro-rata cut if requests exceed it (2025: 60.38% + top-up 14.62% under c. 448).
- **Procedure 2026:** first communication **31/3 – 30/5/2026**; integrative communication **3/1 – 17/1/2027** (mandatory, with e-invoices and certificazione contabile). Agenzia provv. 30/1/2026 n. 3882. Same scheme for ZLS (DL 60/2024 art. 13), EUR 100 m/year.
- **Source:** DL 124/2023 art. 16 as amended by L. 199/2025 c. 438-447; DM 17/5/2024.
- **URLs:** Confindustria 03/2026 pp. 58-61 ; https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/provvedimento-del-30-gennaio-20261
- **Status:** active 2026-2028. **Lever now:** the 2026 window for the first communication is closed; the integrative communication in January 2027 is mandatory. **Conf.: H**.

- **Who:** micro, small and medium enterprises (PMI) buying new capital goods with a bank loan or leasing from participating intermediaries.

- **Status 2026:** active; refinanced EUR 650 m (200 m 2026, 450 m 2027) by L. 199/2025 c. 468; MIMIT notice 24/3/2026; desk opens until funds run out.

### 2.8 IRES premiale (L. 207/2024 art. 1 c. 436-444; DM MEF 8/8/2025, GU n. 190 of 18/8/2025)
- **Who:** IRES subjects; excluded: companies in liquidation, flat-rate regimes, simplified accounting in 2024.
- **Size:** IRES **20%** instead of 24% for **FY2025 only** (solar-year companies; claimed in Redditi SC 2026).
- **Conditions:** ≥80% of 2024 profit set aside; investments in 4.0/5.0 goods ≥ the highest of 30% of the set-aside profit, 24% of 2023 profit, EUR 20,000, **made from 1/1/2025 to the filing deadline of the FY2025 return** (i.e. 2/11/2026 for solar-year companies); average FTE not below the previous 3-year average and +1% permanent staff (at least +1 unit) vs 2024; no CIG in 2024 or 2025 (exceptions). Clawback if the reserve is distributed within the second following year or goods sold within 5 years.
- **Status 2026:** **not renewed** for FY2026 (ordinary IRES 24%, art. 77 TUIR). **Lever now:** last useful moment for FY2025 is the Redditi SC 2026 deadline, **2/11/2026**.
