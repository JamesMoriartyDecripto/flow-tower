# Physics Notes: Mechanics

Flow Tower is not only for agents: any flow you want to study fits. This tower holds school notes for first-biennium mechanics at an Italian liceo scientifico. The top layer is the method for solving an exercise, and its choice sends you to one of the chapters below. Every node opens the notes page of its chapter.

```bash
node bin/flow-tower.js examples/physics-notes
```

## Layers

| Layer | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Solving a Problem | Data → sketch → choose an approach → solve → check units and magnitude (loops back on a wrong unit) |
| Kinematics | Position, velocity, acceleration; uniform, uniformly accelerated motion and free fall |
| Newton's Laws | Forces, third-law pairs, net force; `ΣF = 0?` picks the first or the second law |
| Work & Energy | Work, power, work-energy theorem, potential energy; conserved or lost mechanical energy |

Cross-layer links show where chapters feed each other: the second law gives the acceleration of uniformly accelerated motion, free fall and potential energy share `g`.
