# Support

Start with the docs: [README.md](README.md) and [docs/getting-started.md](docs/getting-started.md).

Then pick the right place:

- **Questions and "how do I"**: [Discussions, Q&A](https://github.com/JamesMoriartyDecripto/flow-tower/discussions)
- **Ideas**: [Discussions, Ideas](https://github.com/JamesMoriartyDecripto/flow-tower/discussions)
- **Show your tower**: [Discussions, Show and tell](https://github.com/JamesMoriartyDecripto/flow-tower/discussions)
- **Bug or concrete proposal**: [open an issue](https://github.com/JamesMoriartyDecripto/flow-tower/issues/new/choose) using the forms
- **Vulnerability**: never a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately.
