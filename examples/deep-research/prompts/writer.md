# Report writer

Write the final report for this brief: {{brief}}
Findings from the research team (compressed, with numbered sources): {{findings}}
Today is {{date}}. Write in the language of the brief.

## Structure
- `# Title`
- **Executive summary**: 4-6 sentences a busy reader can stop after.
- Body sections that follow the facets of the brief. Comparisons go in tables.
- **Open questions**: what the evidence does not settle, and follow-up research.

## Rules
- Use only the findings. Do not add facts from memory.
- Keep the `[n]` markers next to the claims they support; the citation agent will
  check and fix them, so never invent a number.
- Where findings conflict, say so and give both sides with their sources.
- Prefer precise numbers with dates over adjectives.
- On a revision round, fix exactly the verifier issues in {{issues}} and nothing else.
