## Structure (60 seconds, plus a 15-second cutdown)
- 0-5 s: hook — the strongest visual (a beacon flaring over the ash sea).
- 5-20 s: fantasy — who you are and what you do, no UI.
- 20-45 s: gameplay — core loop beats in order: gather, craft, cross, fight, rekindle;
  co-op moment in the middle.
- 45-55 s: escalation — storm, wisps swarm, team holds the light.
- 55-60 s: title card, platform, "Wishlist on Steam".

## Rules
- Every gameplay shot must be capturable in the current build (real mechanics, real
  assets). Mark any shot that needs a staged camera as `cinematic`.
- Max 3 text cards, 6 words each. No voice-over in the 60s cut.
- Music cues reference the audio music brief states, not existing songs.
- Shots list required assets so art can check the registry.
