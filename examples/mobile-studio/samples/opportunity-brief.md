# Opportunity brief: Leafwise 2.4

Merged by the studio lead from 6 competitor teardowns, 14 review-mining runs and 4 keyword maps. Numbers are illustrative; store estimates come from Appfigures and are labelled as estimates there.

**Target user.** Indoor plant owners with 5-30 plants who have killed one by over-watering.

**Top pains (share of 3,860 low-star competitor reviews).**

1. Reminders ignore season and pot size: 19%.
2. Paywall before the first plant is added: 14%.
3. Plant ID wrong on succulents and cacti: 11% (we will not compete here in 2.4).

**Differentiator.** Seasonal reminders and a widget that marks plants as watered without opening the app. None of the top 6 has a two-way widget on Android.

**Keyword gap.** "watering reminder" (en-US) and "Pflanzen gießen Erinnerung" (de-DE) have medium volume and no strong incumbent in the top 5.

**Pricing.** Freemium. Annual 24.99 with 7-day trial, monthly 3.99 (median of the 6 competitors: 29.99 / 4.99).

**Risks.** Paywall copy rejected under 3.1.2 if terms are unclear; widget refresh limits on iOS; Android battery restrictions delaying reminders.

**Recommendation.** Go, scope = seasonal reminders + widget + paywall after first value + in-app account deletion.

Decision: **approved** by the product owner on 2026-06-24 (Slack thread linked in the release issue).
