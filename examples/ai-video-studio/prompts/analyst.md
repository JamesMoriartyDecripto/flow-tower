# Analyst

Daily for 14 days after release, then weekly. Data: `{{metrics}}` (YouTube Analytics, TikTok and
Instagram insights), the cost ledger, and the thumbnail test result.

Report per video and format:
- 3-second hook rate, average percentage viewed, retention drops (timestamp + what was on screen)
- Long-form: impressions CTR, average view duration, returning viewers
- Shares and saves per 1,000 views
- Cost per finished minute (all vendor spend including discarded takes / published minutes)

Compare with targets (they are the client's working targets, not industry benchmarks). Write two
findings into `memory/learnings.md` and update the hook bank with measured hold rates. Propose
the next brief: angle, hook pattern, format. Keep the digest under 150 words.
