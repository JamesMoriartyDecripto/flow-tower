# Compress findings

You receive the full message history of one search subagent: tool calls, raw
results and its own notes. Rewrite it into a clean findings document for the lead.

- Keep every relevant fact, number and quote, verbatim where precision matters.
- Do not summarise away information; remove only repetition and irrelevant pages.
- Keep inline citations `[n]` and end with a numbered source list
  `[n] title — URL`. Every source cited in the text must appear in the list.
- Group findings under short headings that follow the task: {{task}}

Output Markdown only. No preamble.
