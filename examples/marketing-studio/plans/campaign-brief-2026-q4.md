# Campaign brief — Q4 2026 (approved 2026-10-07, CMO + FP&A)

**Quarter goal:** 4.2 M EUR qualified pipeline (SQL value), blended CAC <= 4,500 EUR.
**Media:** 120,000 EUR/month cap, split in `plans/budget-plan-2026-q4.yaml`.
**Markets:** DE (55 %), NL (25 %), FR (20 %). Languages: DE, NL, FR, EN for NL tech buyers.

## C1 — "Stop re-keying" (pillar 1, AP lead + controller)
- **Objective:** demo requests from Fit A/B accounts.
- **Offer:** 20-minute demo with your own supplier invoices.
- **Channels:** Google Search (non-brand: "invoice capture SAP", "Eingangsrechnung automatisieren"),
  LinkedIn (job function Accounting, seniority Manager+, company size 200-2,000), retargeting on Meta.
- **KPIs:** demo CVR >= 4.5 % on the landing page, cost per MQL <= 420 EUR, MQL->SQL >= 35 %.
- **Tests:** short vs long demo form (GrowthBook, `cro/ab-test-plan-demo-page.md`);
  "accuracy" vs "time saved" headline on LinkedIn.

## C2 — "E-invoicing ready" (pillar 1 + C-004, controller + ERP owner)
- **Objective:** content-led demand ahead of e-invoicing deadlines.
- **Offer:** e-invoicing readiness checklist (ungated) + webinar (gated, double opt-in in DE).
- **Channels:** SEO cluster "e-invoicing", LinkedIn document ads, email to consented contacts.
- **KPIs:** organic sessions to cluster +30 % QoQ, webinar registrations 600, assisted pipeline.
- **Guardrail:** compliance statements only from the claims register; legal review on every asset.

## C3 — "Grow without hiring" (pillar 3, CFO)
- **Objective:** CFO-level awareness in Fit A accounts already in pipeline.
- **Channels:** LinkedIn thought-leader ads, Meta (CFO lookalike off consented customer list),
  TikTok awareness test (8 k EUR/month, experimental).
- **KPIs:** account engagement on target list, assisted pipeline; TikTok is killed if no
  assisted pipeline by week 8.

## Lifecycle

- Nurture for non-MQL leads, max 3 marketing emails / 7 days.
