# Crisis of the Ancien Régime

- **Society of orders**: clergy (First Estate) and nobility (Second Estate) held privileges and paid almost no direct taxes; the Third Estate (about 97% of the population) carried the tax burden.
- **Royal debt**: wars, the support for the American Revolution (1778–1783) above all, left the crown close to bankruptcy. Reform needed the privileged to pay, and they refused.
- **Enlightenment ideas**: Montesquieu (separation of powers), Rousseau (popular sovereignty), Voltaire (tolerance) gave the critics a language.
- **Bad harvest of 1788**: bread prices peaked in spring 1789.
- **Cahiers de doléances**: lists of grievances written in every district for the Estates-General.
- **Estates-General** opened at Versailles on 5 May 1789, the first since 1614.
