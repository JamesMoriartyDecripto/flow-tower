# Dynamics: Newton's laws

- **Forces** are vectors, measured in newtons: $$1\ \text{N} = 1\ \text{kg·m/s}^2$$.
- **Common forces**: weight $$P = m\,g$$; normal force (perpendicular to the surface); kinetic friction $$F = \mu\,N$$; spring (Hooke) $$F = -k\,x$$.
- **Net force** $$\sum \vec F$$: the vector sum of every force on the body.

1. **First law (inertia)**: if $$\sum \vec F = 0$$, the body stays at rest or keeps moving at constant velocity.
2. **Second law**: the acceleration has the direction of the net force.

$$
\sum \vec F = m\,\vec a
$$

3. **Third law**: forces come in pairs, equal and opposite, acting on *different* bodies, so they never cancel on the same body.

$$
\vec F_{AB} = -\vec F_{BA}
$$
